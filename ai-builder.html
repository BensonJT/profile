Why this page

Learning to build the way I learned everything else.

I spent thirty-one years at Verizon getting pulled into problems nobody owned, and every fix ended the same way: get what the experts know out of their heads and into something the team can run without them. AI changes the form that takes, so I’m learning it the only way I know how, hands-on, one build at a time. I came into the program already building; what it gives me is the discipline of documenting each build so someone else could run it, and a few things that are new to me. This page is where I keep track.
