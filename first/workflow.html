How information flows through AdoptiScope

The five steps in order, the round-by-round loop, and every dependency between tabs.

Information flow

Inputs on the left, views on the right.

Information flows one way through the five steps, then loops back each round. The inputs you type sit on the left; the views on the right only read.

Each new round, re-score the Sweep, re-open Confirm for any population whose barrier moved, update Root and the Plan, and switch the current round in F1 Setup. The views follow on their own.
