Forester

Process modeling software · product, not publicly launched

2025 – present

Situation
Process models drive redesigns and cost cases, but most are drawn by hand as pictures.
Obstacle
Hand-drawn models are usually wrong and nobody can tell: two people describe the same handoff differently, inputs appear from nowhere, and at scale no reviewer can hold the whole graph in mind.
Action
Built software that captures a process as data, using my decomposition and ICOM method, and draws every diagram from it. A Coach finds contradictions across the model, ranks the fixes, and can complete a missing flow in one click.
Result
One model produces 6 diagram languages and 35 metrics across 6 stakeholder lenses. Phase 1 is feature-complete and in use on a live client engagement.

35

Requirements Fit Analyzer

AI evaluation system · in daily use

2025 – present

Situation
Matching requirement documents against a verified record of evidence is slow, and the first job here was my own career search: every open role, every night.
Obstacle
AI judges are unmeasured by default. A confident wrong score looks the same as a right one, and generated claims drift from the facts.
Action
Rules, trained models and a local embedding model do the volume work. Two AI judges read only the shortlist, and only after a measurement-system study against grades I gave blind. Claims are selected word for word from 202 verified statements, and a script checks every figure.
Result
The fit models reach 0.955 AUC on employers held out of training. One AI judge passed its study; the other failed and is shown only as evidence. My calls outrank every model.

91%
Of my own fails the AI judge caught, measured before it could demote anything
202
Verified statements, each used word for word

Meridian

AI-assisted research system · paid client engagement, in daily use

2026 – present

Situation
A private investor needed an AI-assisted research system. He offered the contract after watching me build and ship Benson Academy.
Obstacle
More than 20 candidate data sources to choose from, and later a nightly transform that ran 7 hours with nobody knowing where the time went.
Action
Delivered and invoiced discovery first: requirements, source evaluation and architecture. Modeled the client’s process in Forester, and that model became the architecture. Profiled the transform instead of guessing, then fixed the write path: narrower rows, an autovacuum gate, and a query that reads 100× fewer pages.
Result
In daily use on the client’s machine since August 2026. The nightly window fell from 7 hours to 4. A faster-looking rewrite measured 3.3× slower and stayed behind a flag.

Benson Academy

Generative AI content pipeline · in production

2025 – present

Situation
A multi-course Bible curriculum where every lesson must be faithful to its sources, published in many formats.
Obstacle
Generative AI produces fluent errors, and one wrong quotation undermines the whole lesson.
Action
Built the full-stack Next.js and TypeScript site solo, and a 13-phase production process written as skills Claude follows. Every step that touches Scripture pulls it from a translation tool, a script checks every quotation, and a human review gate comes before publication.
Result
More than 40 published assets per lesson, from study guides and slide decks to a podcast and section videos. Research-to-published cycle time fell about 80%, with every source traceable.

~80%

13

The same shape any organization needs before it puts generative AI near customer-facing or regulated content: grounded in a source of record, with a person accountable at the gate.

Data migration validation

Work sample · public and runnable

2025

Situation
A take-home assessment for a government data engineering role: compare a legacy healthcare claims system with its replacement and say whether the migration should proceed.
Obstacle
The headline quality numbers looked good: 4.58σ on carrier claims and 4.68σ on beneficiary summary.
Action
Ran it as a quality problem: defects per million, sigma levels and yield, field by field, rolled into a one-screen go/no-go scorecard. Built with Claude Code on DuckDB, SQLAlchemy, scipy and a 9-view Streamlit dashboard. The analysis and framing were mine.
Result
Recommended do not proceed: 4,777 claims and 159 beneficiaries were missing outright and 82,699 payments disagreed. Good average quality was hiding categorical failures.

sheets_decomposer

Spreadsheet analysis utility · open source

2026

Situation
Every operation has a critical spreadsheet built by someone who left: twenty tabs, no documentation, and no time to untangle it.
Obstacle
Reading it cell by cell is how people start, and why they never finish.
Action
Built a tool that extracts the whole workbook as data: formulas, references, dependencies between tabs. It collapses repeated formulas to one line, classifies each tab, and runs the defect checks a careful analyst would.
Result
A defect report, a queryable database, and a context file that turns an AI assistant into living documentation that cites the cell. The model never sees the data, and the checks were scored against planted defects before they were trusted.
