The approach

Follow the work, not the org chart.

Most transformations don’t fail on ambition. They fail in the handoffs, between teams, between systems, between the plan and the people who run it. I find those seams, fix them with the people who do the work, and leave something that stays standing.

I’m Jeffrey Benson. I look at how work moves from the first request to the finished result: where it waits, where people redo it, and where nobody is sure who makes the call.

How it works

What working together looks like

01

Listen first.See the whole.

I start with the people who carry the weight. We map what is happening and trace the work across the teams. We find where it waits and where it lands with someone who can’t move it forward.

02

Fix the foundation.Agree on what better means.

I collect and validate the data and definitions the work depends on. Before we change anything, we agree on what should improve and how we’ll tell whether it did.

03

Leave it running.

Build the change with the people who will run it. Before I leave, the team knows who owns it and what to do if performance slips. I count it a success when it keeps working after I’ve gone.
