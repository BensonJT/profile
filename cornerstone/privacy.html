Privacy

How your message is handled

I collect only what you choose to send me.

What I collect

If you use the contact form, I receive your name, your email address and your message, along with technical details such as your IP address and the time you sent it. The page asks for nothing else. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts.

How your message travels

The form sends your message to Web3Forms, a form service that emails it to me. Web3Forms stores submissions encrypted on Amazon Web Services and deletes them automatically after up to three years. To filter spam it shares the sender’s IP address and email address with two spam-filtering services, CleanTalk and Akismet. It also relies on Cloudflare and Hetzner, and it is operated from India, so your message may be processed outside your own country. The details are in the Web3Forms privacy policy.

How I use it

I use your message only to reply to you. I do not sell it, share it, add you to a mailing list or use it for advertising. I keep it in my email for as long as the conversation is useful and delete it when you ask.

Booking a call

The Book a time button takes you to cal.com. Anything you enter there is handled under the cal.com privacy policy.

Hosting

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. My main site has its own privacy page.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete anything I hold about you. Message me on LinkedIn and I will answer within a month. I will also pass deletion requests on to Web3Forms. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal data, and this is how you use them.

Children

This page is written for business use and is not directed to children.

Questions

Message me on LinkedIn. If this policy changes, I will update this page and its date.
