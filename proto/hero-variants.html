How to judge these

Run the five-second test on each variant separately. Show one variant to someone who does not know your work, for five seconds, then ask two questions: what does he do for a living, and what would you hire him for. A variant passes when the answer contains a job-shaped noun and the person is still curious. Do not show the same person two variants; first impressions do not reset.

Trade-offs. A keeps maximum continuity with LinkedIn and the résumé summary, which now carry the "nobody owns" theme.
